Privacy · 隱私

Lexigraph is a static reader. It has no accounts and does not upload your library to a Lexigraph server.

Lexigraph 是純靜態的閱讀器。沒有帳號，也不會把書庫上傳到 Lexigraph 的伺服器。

What stays in this browser · 留在瀏覽器裡

  • PDFs, word familiarity, notes, review schedule, and phrase bookmarks live in IndexedDB on this device.
  • 你打開的 PDF、單字熟悉度、筆記、複習排程和片語都在這台裝置的 IndexedDB。
  • Gemini and Groq API keys, if you save them, live in localStorage. Backups leave them out unless you opt in.
  • 若你填了 API key，它只在 localStorage。備份預設不含 key。

AI calls · AI 請求

When you ask for an explanation, your browser calls the provider you chose — Google Gemini, Groq, or Ollama on your own machine. Lexigraph does not proxy or store those requests.

你要求解釋時，瀏覽器直接連到你選的服務（Gemini、Groq，或你自己的 Ollama）。Lexigraph 不代轉，也不保存這些請求。

Clearing this site’s data in the browser deletes the library. Export a backup first.

清除這個網站的瀏覽器資料會刪掉書庫。請先匯出備份。
